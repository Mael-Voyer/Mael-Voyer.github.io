# Code development
- *TauREx:* I participate in the development of TauREx, focusing on implementing new functionnilites to better handle atmospheric spectra of directly imaged exoplanets.
- *TauREx-PCQ:* This plugin enables to perform *fast* retrieval with as many clouds species as desired (20 times speed gain for 4 clouds). Based on pre-computed grids to remove on-the-fly Mie calculation it enables to easely runs retrievals with 5+ different cloud species. 
- *TauREx-disk:* With JWST several low-mass planetary objects have been observed half embeded in circumplanetary disk. This plugin enables to couple retrieval of the atmosphere and of the circumplanetary disk.
 
 ---

 - *JWST:* The James Webb Space Telescope is the new great explorer of our universe and propusled exoplanet research in to a new era. I use JWST to explore the atmosphere of various directly imaged exoplanets aimaing at populations level studies.
 - *ARIEL:* I joined the ARIEL consortium and have been invole in preparing retrieval tools for the mission. ARIEL will be esential to understand the physics of exoplanet atmospheres by providing spectroscopy of several hundreds exoplanets.
